# Code of conduct

AquaRecover should be a place where people can discuss software without being
belittled or harassed.

Be direct about technical problems and considerate toward the people involved.
Disagreement is fine; insults, threats, discrimination, sexual attention,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are not. Do not publish another person's
private information or test media without permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that violate these rules. Repeated or serious violations may lead
to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct problems privately through the maintainer contact shown on the
GitHub profile rather than opening a public issue. Include links or screenshots
that help establish what happened. Reports will be reviewed with the privacy of
the people involved in mind.
